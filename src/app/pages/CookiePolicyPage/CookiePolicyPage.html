Cookies Policy

This app uses browser storage for a few small, functional features such as remembering your favourite cards.

What are cookies here?

In this app, we use local browser storage rather than traditional advertising cookies. This is used to remember your user preferences across page refreshes.

Why we use them

We use local storage to keep your favourites list available, so you do not lose your saved study items after reloading the page.

Managing preferences

You can remove this stored data at any time from your browser settings or by clearing site data for this app.
