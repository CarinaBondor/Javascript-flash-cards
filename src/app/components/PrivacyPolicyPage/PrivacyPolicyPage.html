Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This app stores only the information needed to provide a functional learning experience.

What we store

The app may save your favourite flash card selections in your browser using local storage. This helps keep your list of favourites between visits.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data, do not track your browsing activity for advertising, and do not require account creation to use the app.

Local storage

Some preferences, such as favourited cards, may be stored locally on your device. You can clear this data from your browser settings at any time.
